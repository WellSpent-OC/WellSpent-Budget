# Use WellSpent without an account

> *Play solo, at one table.*

You don't need an account to use WellSpent on one device. Signed out, the bottom of the sidebar says **Kept on this computer only**, and your budgets stay on that computer. On an iPhone, the bottom of Home says **Kept on this iPhone only**.

You need an account to sync between your devices, and to share a group. When you make one, the groups you made come into it. [Quest 2: Make your account](make-your-account.md)
