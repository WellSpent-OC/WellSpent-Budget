# Quest 6: Bring in your first statement

> *Carry your gold home from the bank, and give each coin a budget.*

**Before you start:** a statement file from your bank, for this month. In your bank's download menu, pick "CSV" (comma-separated values), "OFX" (Open Financial Exchange) or "QFX" (Quicken Financial Exchange). Not QuickBooks (QBO). WellSpent can't read a PDF. In a shared group, you need the Add level or higher.

There's no bank connection. You download a statement from your bank and bring it in, so your bank login stays with you.

1. Click a budget in the group the statement is for.
2. Press **Import statement**. On a Mac, it may show as an icon only.
3. Click **Downloads**.
4. Double-click your statement file.
5. For each line marked **Needs a budget**, pick a budget from its menu. To skip a line, leave it on **Choose**.
6. Press **Import**.

   > You see the new lines in your budgets, marked **Statement**.

Skip deposits, transfers and payments to your card: leave them on **Choose**.

## If it doesn't work

- **WellSpent can't read the file:** download it again from your bank as another type, and try again.
- **A line is missing:** a budget's page shows this month only. Lines from earlier months go in, but don't show there.
- **You imported the same file twice:** nothing is added twice. Lines already imported are skipped.
- **A purchase you typed in shows twice:** the amounts were different, or the dates were more than four days apart.
