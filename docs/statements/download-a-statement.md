# Side quest: Download a statement from your bank

> *First, fetch the scroll from the bank.*

There's no bank connection. You download a statement from your bank yourself, so your bank login stays with you. Every bank's website is a little different, so these steps are in words.

1. Sign in to your bank's website, and open the account you spend from.
2. Find the option to download transactions or statements.
3. Pick dates for this month.
4. Pick one of these file types: "CSV" or "Excel/CSV" (comma-separated values), "OFX" (Open Financial Exchange), or "QFX" or "Quicken". Not QuickBooks (QBO), and not PDF.
5. Download it.

   > You see the file in your **Downloads** folder.

## On Linux

On Linux, the steps are the same as on a Mac.

Then bring it in: [Quest 6: Bring in your first statement](bring-in-your-first-statement.md)
