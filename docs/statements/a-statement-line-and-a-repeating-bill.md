# Side quest: A statement line and a repeating bill

**Coming in the next update.**

> *Two cards for one cost? Only one stays on the table.*

When you bring in a statement, a line that matches a repeating bill takes the bill's place. That way the bill counts once.

**Before you start:** a statement to bring in. See [Quest 6: Bring in your first statement](bring-in-your-first-statement.md).

1. Bring in the statement, as in Quest 6.

2. In the review, look for rows under **Matched** that say **Replaces**, such as "Replaces December's Phone bill, $85.00".

3. To keep the bill and the line, press **Keep both** on that row.

4. Press **Import**.

   > You see the bank's line in the budget, in the bill's place.

The bank's line keeps the bill's budget, and its house if it had one.

**What counts as a match:** a bill a repeat made, in the same budget, dated within 4 days of the line. The amounts must be within 10 percent or $5, whichever is more. If two bills fit, the closest date wins.

## On an iPhone

**Coming in the next update.**

The steps are the same, except **Keep both** is a switch on the row. Turn it on, and the row says **Will be added**. Turn it off again, and the line goes back to replacing the bill.

## On Linux

The steps are the same as on a Mac.

## If it doesn't work

- **A bill counts twice:** the bill and the line were further apart than a match allows. Delete one copy.
