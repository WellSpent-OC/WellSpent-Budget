# Tour: A group's page

> *Meet your party.*

Click a group's name in the sidebar, or pick **People…** from its menu, and its page opens. Shown here as an Admin sees it.

![A group's page on a Mac, with numbers 1 to 19](../assets/shots/tour-a-groups-page.svg)

1. **Home**: the group's name.
2. **Sync**, the two arrows: send and get changes now.
3. **Account**, the person: your account, and signing out.
4. **Invite Someone…**: share the group. [Quest 7](share-a-group.md)
5. The line under the name: whether it's shared, how many people, and who made it.
6. **MONEY**: the whole group's month, every budget added up.
7. The group's wheel: what's left this month across the group's budgets. Its middle says **Left this month**, and of how much. Over, it's orange all the way round and says **Over this month**.
8. The tick: where the green would end today, at an even pace.
9. **Spent**: what the group has spent this month.
10. **Left**: what's left across its budgets.
11. **Expected left by today**: what would be left today, at an even pace.
12. **Day 18 of 31. Every budget in Home, added up.**: how far into the month, and what the wheel counts.
13. **PEOPLE**: everyone in the group.
14. **Owner**: the person who made the group.
15. **(you)**: your own row, with your level as a label. It has no **Remove…**
16. The line under a name: what that person's level lets them do.
17. The level list: change someone's level. Only the Owner or an Admin can make someone an Admin.
18. **Remove…**: take someone out of the group. What they added stays. It asks first.
19. **Leave Group…**: leave the group. The others keep it. The person who made a group deletes it instead.

A group with no budgets shows no MONEY. At the Add or View level, the page shows each person's level as a label, with **Leave Group…**

## On an iPhone

Tap a group's name on Home, and its page opens. Shown here as an Admin sees it.

![A group's page on an iPhone, with numbers 1 to 9](../assets/shots/tour-iphone-group.svg)

1. The back arrow: go back to Home.
2. **⋯**: the group's menu, with items such as **Share** and **Rename**.
3. The line under the name: whether it's shared, how many people, and who made it.
4. **Robin**: a person in the group. Your own row says **(you)**.
5. The line under a name: what that person's level lets them do.
6. **Owner**: their level. The Owner is the person who made the group.
7. The arrow: open that person, to change their level or remove them. It shows only on rows you may change. An Admin's own row has one too, to change their own level.
8. **Leave Group**: leave the group. The others keep it. The person who made a group deletes it instead.
9. **Invite Someone**: share the group. It shows at the Manage level or higher. [Quest 7](share-a-group.md)

The iPhone's group page has no MONEY wheel. Each budget's wheel is on its own page.

## On an iPhone: a person

Tap the arrow on a person's row, and their page opens.

![A person's page on an iPhone, with numbers 1 to 5](../assets/shots/tour-iphone-person.svg)

1. The back arrow: go back to the group's page.
2. **View**: a level, under **Can**. Tap a level to change theirs at once.
3. The line under a level: what it lets them do.
4. The check: their level now.
5. **Remove**: take them out of the group. What they added stays. It asks first.

You see only the levels you may give. Only the Owner or an Admin can make someone an Admin.

## On Linux

The page is the same as on a Mac, except **Sync** and **Account** are at the bottom of the sidebar, not on the page.
