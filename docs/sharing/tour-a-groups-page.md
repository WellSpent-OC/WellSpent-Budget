# Tour: A group's page

> *Meet your party.*

**Coming in the next update:** on a Mac, click a group's name in the sidebar, or pick **People…** from its menu, and its page opens. Shown here as an Admin sees it.

![A group's page on a Mac, with numbers 1 to 10](../assets/shots/tour-a-groups-page.svg)

1. **Home**: the group's name.
2. **Invite Someone…**: share the group. [Quest 7](share-a-group.md)
3. The line under the name: whether it's shared, how many people, and who made it.
4. **PEOPLE**: everyone in the group.
5. **Owner**: the person who made the group.
6. **(you)**: your own row, with your level as a label. It has no **Remove…**
7. The line under a name: what that person's level lets them do.
8. The level list: change someone's level. Only the Owner or an Admin can make someone an Admin.
9. **Remove…**: take someone out of the group. What they added stays. It asks first.
10. **Leave Group…**: leave the group. The others keep it. The person who made a group deletes it instead.

At the Add or View level, the page shows each person's level as a label, with **Leave Group…**
