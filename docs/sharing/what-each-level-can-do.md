# What each level can do

> *Every party has its roles.*

Everyone in a shared group has a level. It sets what they can do in that group.

- **View:** sees every budget and transaction. Changes nothing.
- **Add:** adds transactions and imports statements, and changes or deletes their own transactions.
- **Manage:** also makes and changes budgets, changes or deletes anyone's transactions, and invites other people at View or Add.
- **Admin:** can do what a manager does, and make or remove other managers and admins.
- **Owner:** whoever made the group. The Owner can do everything.

Only the Owner or an Admin can delete a group for everyone. Everyone else can leave it.

When you share a group, you pick View, Add or Manage. To change someone's level later, see [Side quest: Change someone's level, or remove them](change-someones-level.md).

## On Linux

The levels are the same on Linux.
