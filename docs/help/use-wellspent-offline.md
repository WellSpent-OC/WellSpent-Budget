# Use WellSpent offline

WellSpent keeps your budgets on your Mac, iPhone or Linux computer, so it keeps working with no internet. Changes you make offline wait. They go out at the first sync after you're back online. On an iPhone, that sync runs only while WellSpent is open: within two minutes, when you come back to the app, or right away if you pull Home down.

With no internet, a sync stops after about 30 seconds and says it couldn't reach the server. It tries again later. Press **Sync** to try again right away. On an iPhone, pull Home down.
