# Use WellSpent offline

WellSpent keeps your budgets on your Mac, iPhone or Linux computer, so it keeps working with no internet. Changes you make offline wait. They go out at the first sync after you're back online. On an iPhone, that sync runs only while WellSpent is open: within two minutes, when you come back to the app, or right away if you pull Home down. On Linux, it runs every two minutes while WellSpent is open, or right away when you press **Sync** at the bottom of the sidebar.

With no connection at all, a sync stops right away and says this computer, or this iPhone, is not connected to the internet. On a network that never answers, such as Wi-Fi with no internet behind it, it stops after about 30 seconds and says "Could not reach the server. Check its address and your connection." The syncs that run on their own say nothing when they fail, and try again later. Press **Sync** to try right away and see why it failed. On an iPhone, pull Home down: the reason shows in the line at the foot of Home, between **Account** and **+**.
