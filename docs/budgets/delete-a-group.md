# Side quest: Delete a group

> *Pack up the whole camp.*

**Before you start:** only the person who made the group, or an Admin, can delete it. A shared group is deleted for everyone in it. This can't be undone.

1. ![A hand right-clicks the group's name in the sidebar](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-1-mac.svg)
   Right-click the group's name in the sidebar.

2. ![A hand picks Delete](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-2.svg)
   Pick **Delete…**

   > You see a question that names the group, and how many budgets and transactions go with it.

3. ![A hand presses Delete in the question](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-3.svg)
   Press **Delete** in the question.

   > You see the group gone from your sidebar.

## On an iPhone

1. ![A thumb holds the group's name on Home](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-iphone-1.svg)
   Touch and hold the group's name on Home.

2. ![A thumb taps Delete](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-iphone-2.svg)
   Tap **Delete**.

3. ![A thumb taps Delete in the question](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-iphone-3.svg)
   Tap **Delete** in the question.

## On Linux

1. ![Press ⋯ beside the group's name in the sidebar](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-linux-1.svg)
   Press **⋯** beside the group's name in the sidebar.

2. ![Pick Delete](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-linux-2.svg)
   Pick **Delete…**

   > You see a question that names the group, and how many budgets and transactions go with it.

3. ![Press Delete in the question](../assets/shots/delete-a-group-linux-3.svg)
   Press **Delete** in the question.

   > You see the group gone from your sidebar.

## If it doesn't work

- **The menu says Leave…, not Delete…:** someone else made the group. You can leave it. [Side quest: Leave a group](../sharing/leave-a-group.md)
