# Tour: The budget window

> *Every pouch gets a name and a size.*

**Add Budget**, or **New Budget in Home…** on a group's menu, opens this window.

![The New Budget window, with numbers 1 to 6](../assets/shots/tour-the-budget-window.svg)

1. **Name**: what the budget is for, like Groceries.
2. **Each month**: how much to spend each month.
3. **Group**: the group it goes in.
4. **Starts again on the 1st of each month.**: when the budget fills up again.
5. **Cancel**: close, and make nothing.
6. **Add**: make the budget. It stays dimmed until there's a name and an amount above zero.

**Edit…** on a budget's menu opens the same window, called **Edit Budget**, with **Save** in place of **Add**, and no **Group**.

## On an iPhone

**New Budget** under a group on Home, or **Add Budget** in the **+** menu, opens the same fields in a sheet. **Cancel** is at the top left, and **Add** at the top right. **Group** always shows for a new budget: pick from the groups you manage, with the group you started from already chosen. With no group to put it in, **Group** is a box with Personal filled in, and **Add** makes that group too. **Edit** on a budget opens it as **Edit Budget**, with **Save**, and a red **Delete** at the bottom.
