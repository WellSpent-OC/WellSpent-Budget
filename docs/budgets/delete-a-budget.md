# Side quest: Delete a budget

> *Empty a pouch for good.*

**Before you start:** you need the Manage level or higher. A budget's transactions are deleted with it. This can't be undone.

1. ![A hand right-clicks a budget in the sidebar](../assets/shots/delete-a-budget-1-mac.svg)
   Right-click the budget in the sidebar.

2. ![A hand picks Delete](../assets/shots/delete-a-budget-2.svg)
   Pick **Delete…**

   > You see a question that names the budget, and how many transactions go with it.

3. ![A hand presses Delete in the question](../assets/shots/delete-a-budget-3.svg)
   Press **Delete** in the question.

## On an iPhone

1. ![A thumb holds a budget on Home](../assets/shots/delete-a-budget-iphone-1.svg)
   Touch and hold the budget on Home.

2. ![A thumb taps Delete](../assets/shots/delete-a-budget-iphone-2.svg)
   Tap **Delete**.

3. ![A thumb taps Delete in the question](../assets/shots/delete-a-budget-iphone-3.svg)
   Tap **Delete** in the question.
