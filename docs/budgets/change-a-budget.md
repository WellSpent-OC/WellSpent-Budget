# Side quest: Change a budget's name or amount

> *A bigger pouch, or a smaller one.*

**Before you start:** you need the Manage level or higher.

1. ![A hand right-clicks a budget in the sidebar](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-1-mac.svg)
   Right-click the budget in the sidebar.

2. ![A hand picks Edit](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-2.svg)
   Pick **Edit…**

3. ![A hand types in Each month](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-3.svg)
   Change **Name**, or **Each month**.

4. ![A hand presses Save](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-4.svg)
   Press **Save**.

   > You see the budget's new name or amount, and what's left follows at once.

When the money wheel has something to say, **Edit Budget…** on the budget's page opens the same window. [Side quest: Change a budget from its wheel](change-a-budget-from-its-wheel.md)

## On an iPhone

1. ![A thumb holds a budget on Home](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-iphone-1.svg)
   Touch and hold the budget on Home.

2. ![A thumb taps Edit](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-iphone-2.svg)
   Tap **Edit**.

3. ![A thumb types in Each month](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-iphone-3.svg)
   Change **Name**, or **Each month**.

4. ![A thumb taps Save](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-iphone-4.svg)
   Tap **Save**.

## On Linux

1. ![Click the budget in the sidebar](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-linux-1.svg)
   Click the budget in the sidebar.

   > You see the budget's page.

2. ![Press Edit, at the top of the page](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-linux-2.svg)
   Press **Edit…**, at the top of the page.

3. ![Change Name, or Each month](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-linux-3.svg)
   Change **Name**, or **Each month**.

4. ![Press Save](../assets/shots/change-a-budget-linux-4.svg)
   Press **Save**.

   > You see the budget's new name or amount, and what's left follows at once.
