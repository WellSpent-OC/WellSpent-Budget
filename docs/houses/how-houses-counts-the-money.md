# How Houses counts the money

> *Know the rules before you play.*

**Houses** lists each group that has a house, with its houses under it. A group with no houses isn't listed.

## A group's page

- **Runway:** about how many months the group can keep going. It divides what the group has on hand by the gap each month between what it spends and its share of the houses' NOI. It says "about", never a date or a promise.
- **Booked:** what the group's houses booked this month.
- **Operating expenses:** commission, plus bills marked with a house.
- **NOI:** net operating income, what a house brings in, less its operating expenses.
- **Next 30 days:** nights booked in the next 30 days.
- A row for each house, with its nights and its NOI.
- **Needs you:** today's turnovers, tomorrow's check-ins, and empty nights between two stays. When there's nothing, it says "Nothing needs you today."

## A house's page

- Its booked amount, expenses, NOI and nights in the next 30 days.
- **Operating expenses:** each line, with where it came from.
- **Booked by month:** a month before the house's first stay shows nothing, not zero.
- **Coming up:** the next stays.
- **Try an improvement**, and **Against your plan and the market**.

A house's page has no wheel and no budget list.

## What counts

- **Booked** counts cleaning fees. A cleaning fee is money in, not an expense.
- **Commission** comes from PriceLabs, or the service fee in an Airbnb file. Where a booking system doesn't send it, the house says **Commission not sent**, and NOI reads higher than it is.
- **Pet fees** are left out, so NOI reads a little low on a stay with a pet fee.
- **A stay never counts as budget spending.** No stay shows in a budget, and no budget total includes one.

## Who sees it

Everyone in the group sees its houses and stays, with no key of their own. They also see whose PriceLabs brought the stays in. Someone who joined "from now on" sees stays from the day they joined, so their numbers can differ.

Your stays and house names sync through WellSpent's server. Everyone in the group can see them. The server can read them. Guest names aren't saved.
