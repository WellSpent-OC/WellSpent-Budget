# Side quest: Connect PriceLabs

> *Call in your scout. PriceLabs reports every stay.*

**Before you start:**

- Properties is on. See [Side quest: Turn on Properties](turn-on-properties.md).
- Your PriceLabs key. In PriceLabs, go to Account Settings, then API Details. It has to be the account owner's key.
- The Manage level in each group a house will join.
- Choose the group with care. Your stays and house names sync through WellSpent's server. Everyone in the group can see them. The server can read them. Guest names aren't saved.

1. On **Houses**, press **Connect PriceLabs**. Once you have a house, it's in **Add House**, under a group's houses.

2. Paste your key into **Your PriceLabs key**.

3. Press **Continue**.

   > You see **Pick your houses**, with your listings.

4. Tick each listing that is one of your houses. Pick one listing per house.

5. For each one, pick a group in **Group**. To make a new one, pick **+ New group** and type its name.

6. Press **Add 2 Houses**. The button counts what you ticked: with one, it says **Add 1 House**. With none ticked, it's dimmed.

   > You see your houses on **Houses**, under their groups.

Adding houses adds nothing to your budgets. A new group starts with no budgets.

- **One listing per house:** the same house on two channels would count its stays twice.
- **Your key stays on your devices.** It never goes to WellSpent's server. On an iPhone or a Mac, your Keychain holds it, and iCloud Keychain can share it between them.
- **WellSpent only reads** your listings and bookings. It never changes anything in PriceLabs.
- **PriceLabs' own charge:** PriceLabs charges $1 a listing a month, plus tax, for listings that sync prices. That's PriceLabs' charge, not WellSpent's.
- **"Commission not sent":** some booking systems, such as Lodgify, don't send their commission. A house's NOI reads higher than it is there.

## On an iPhone

**Coming in the next update.**

1. In the **Houses** tab, tap **Connect PriceLabs**. Once a group has a house, it's in the **…** button at the top right of the group's page. Only a manager sees it there.

2. Tap **Paste your key here**, and paste your key.

3. Tap **Continue**.

4. Tick each listing that is one of your houses.

5. For each one, pick its group.

6. Tap **Add 2 Houses**. The button counts what you ticked.

## On Linux

Properties isn't on Linux yet.

## If it doesn't work

- **"PriceLabs didn't accept that key.":** copy the key again from PriceLabs, and paste it.
- **"That key is a team member's.":** PriceLabs gives bookings only to the account owner's key. Paste the owner's key.
- **"PriceLabs could not be reached.":** try again in a moment.
- **"No bookings yet.":** PriceLabs only has what your booking system sends it. Nothing is broken.
- **A group isn't in the list:** you're below the Manage level in it.
