# Side quest: Add money coming in

> *Loot! Count what comes in, not just what goes out.*

Money in is income, such as pay or a rental payout. It adds to a budget instead of spending from it.

**Before you start:** the Add level or higher in the group.

1. In the budget, press **Add**.

2. Type who paid in **Merchant**, and how much in **Amount**.

3. Turn on **Money in**.

   > You see the line under it, such as "Adds to Bookings instead of spending from it."

4. Press **Add**.

   > You see the entry in the list, with a plus sign.

## How the group's wheel shows it

- Money in fills the group's wheel back up, but never past full.
- The middle says what came in, what went out, and the difference, such as "$1,230 in", "$2,778 out" and "−$1,548", for this month.
- Spent more than the budgets, but money in covers it? The wheel stays green, and says so: "Over the month's budgets by $420.00. Money in covers it."
- Spent more than the budgets and the money in together? The wheel turns orange.
- The tick still marks your spending pace. Money in doesn't move it.
- With no money in this month, the wheel looks the same as always.

## What isn't money in

- **A refund** lowers what a budget spent.
- **Borrowed money** and **money from savings** show on their own lines, such as "$500.00 borrowed this month". They never count as income.
- **A transfer** between your own accounts, and **a card payment**, count as nothing.

From a bank statement, a line that is money in is offered as **Money in**, as **Income** or **Refund**. See [the import review](../statements/tour-the-import-review.md).

## On an iPhone

1. In the budget, tap **+**.

2. Type the **Merchant** and **Amount**.

3. Turn on **Money in**.

4. Tap **Add**.

## On Linux

The steps are the same as on a Mac.
