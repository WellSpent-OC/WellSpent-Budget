# Side quest: Change a repeating bill

> *Rewrite one round, or every round from here on.*

**Before you start:** the person who set the repeat, and anyone at the Manage level, can change one month or every month after. Anyone else who may change the bill can change one month only.

1. Click the month's bill in the budget's list.

2. Change what's different, such as the **Amount**.

3. In **Which months?**, pick **This month only** or **This month and later**.

4. Press **Save**.

   > You see the change in the list.

- **This month only:** only that month changes. Next month goes back to the usual amount.
- **This month and later:** that month and every later month change. Earlier months stay as they were.

## Change it from the list of repeats

Each budget with repeats says how many under its totals, such as **2 repeats**.

1. Press the count line, such as **2 repeats**.

   > You see **Repeats in Utilities**, with each repeat, its day, and its amount.

2. Click the repeat.

3. Change the **Merchant**, the **Amount** or the **Day of the month**.

4. Press **Save**.

A change here reaches this month and every month after. To change one month, open that month's bill.

## On an iPhone

**Coming in the next update.**

1. In the budget, tap the month's bill.

2. Change what's different.

3. In **Which months?**, pick **This month only** or **This month and later**.

4. Tap **Save**.

To change the repeat itself, tap the count line, such as **2 repeats**, then tap the repeat.

## On Linux

The steps are the same as on a Mac.

## If it doesn't work

- **There's no This month and later:** only the person who set the repeat, or a manager, can change later months.
- **The repeat's sheet has nothing to change:** it says who can change it, such as "Only Robin or a manager can change this repeat."
