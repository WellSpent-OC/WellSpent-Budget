# Who can see your budgets

Your budgets sync through WellSpent's server. Connections and backups are encrypted. The server can read your budgets. That's how it checks who may change what, and why resetting a forgotten password doesn't lose your budgets.

## Inside the app

- Only you see a group you haven't shared.
- In a shared group, everyone sees every budget and transaction in it, whatever their level. Each transaction shows who added it.
- People in one group don't see your other groups.
- Someone you invite with **Only from now on** sees the group's budgets, but only the transactions added after they join.
- Budgets you make while signed out stay on that computer only, unless you then make an account on it. Making an account takes them into it.

## Your bank, and the code

WellSpent doesn't connect to your bank, so your bank login stays with you. You bring in a statement you download yourself.

WellSpent's code is open source, so anyone can read it and check what the app does.
