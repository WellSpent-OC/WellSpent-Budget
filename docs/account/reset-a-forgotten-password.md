# Side quest: Reset a forgotten password

> *Lost the key to the vault? The smith can make you a new one.*

**Before you start:** your email must be confirmed. A reset code lasts 30 minutes.

1. ![A hand presses Sign in at the bottom of the sidebar](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-1.svg)
   Press **Sign in** at the bottom of the sidebar.

2. ![A hand types in Email](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-2.svg)
   Type your email in **Email**.

3. ![A hand presses Forgot your password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-3.svg)
   Press **Forgot your password?**

4. ![A hand presses Send code](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-4.svg)
   Press **Send code**.

   > You see that a code is on its way, and that it lasts 30 minutes.

5. ![An email with the subject Reset your WellSpent password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-5.svg)
   Open the email from WellSpent whose subject starts with **Reset your WellSpent password**. The code follows in the subject.

6. ![A hand types the code in Code](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-6.svg)
   Type or paste the code in **Code**.

7. ![A hand types in New password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-7.svg)
   Type a new password of at least 10 characters in **New password**.

8. ![A hand types in Confirm password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-8.svg)
   Type the same password in **Confirm password**.

9. ![A hand presses Set password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-9.svg)
   Press **Set password**.

   > You see "Your password is changed, and you are signed in. Every other device was signed out."

Then sign in again on your other devices. Your budgets stay.

## On an iPhone

1. ![A thumb taps Sign in at the bottom of Home](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-1.svg)
   Tap **Sign in** at the bottom left of Home.

2. ![A thumb types in Email](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-2.svg)
   Type your email in **Email**.

3. ![A thumb taps Forgot your password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-3.svg)
   Tap **Forgot your password?**

4. ![A thumb taps Send code](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-4.svg)
   Tap **Send code**.

5. ![An email with the subject Reset your WellSpent password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-5.svg)
   Open the email whose subject starts with **Reset your WellSpent password**. The code follows in the subject.

6. ![A thumb types the code and a new password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-6.svg)
   Type the code in **Code**, and a new password of at least 10 characters in **New password** and **Confirm password**.

7. ![A thumb taps Set password](../assets/shots/reset-a-forgotten-password-iphone-7.svg)
   Tap **Set password**.

## If it doesn't work

- **No code email:** check your spam or junk folder. A code goes only to an email that has an account and was confirmed. If yours was never confirmed, write to **dev@wellspent.space**.
- **The code ran out:** press **Start over**, then **Send code** again.
