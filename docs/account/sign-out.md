# Side quest: Sign out

> *Pack up your gear. It'll be here when you're back.*

Signing out doesn't delete your budgets. They come back when you sign in again.

1. ![A hand presses Account at the top of a budget's page](../assets/shots/sign-out-1.svg)
   Press **Account**, the person at the top of a budget's page.

2. ![A hand presses Sign out](../assets/shots/sign-out-2.svg)
   Press **Sign out**.

   > You see **Kept on this computer only** at the bottom of the sidebar. Your account's budgets are hidden until you sign in again.

Signing out works even with no internet.

## On an iPhone

1. ![A thumb taps Account at the bottom of Home](../assets/shots/sign-out-iphone-1.svg)
   Tap **Account** at the bottom left of Home.

2. ![A thumb taps Sign out](../assets/shots/sign-out-iphone-2.svg)
   Tap **Sign out**.

   > You see **Kept on this iPhone only** at the bottom of Home.

## On Linux

1. ![A hand presses Account at the bottom of the sidebar](../assets/shots/sign-out-linux-1.svg)
   Press **Account** at the bottom of the sidebar.

2. ![A hand presses Sign out](../assets/shots/sign-out-linux-2.svg)
   Press **Sign out**.

   > You see **Kept on this computer only** at the bottom of the sidebar. Your account's budgets are hidden until you sign in again.
